    Class SkyboxShader

    A procedural sky shader designed for rendering dynamic skyboxes. It can generate a sky with a sun, moon, clouds, and stars, and also supports blending with a cubemap texture.

    Remarks

    This shader is highly configurable through its public properties, allowing for real-time changes to the time of day, weather conditions, and celestial body positions.
