# Omega Run

A side-on platformer made with the engine: Bit, a small round robot, runs and
jumps to the flag at the end of each level. Inspired by the mechanics of the
genre (variable jump height, blocks overhead, stomping enemies, power-ups) with
its own hero, worlds and look. Plan and decisions: the "Omega Plataformas: plano" doc.
3D drawn from the side; the hero has its own physics on a tile grid (no Rapier),
so the game stays small and its tests run in Node.
`npm run build:games -- platformer` bundles it into `docs/games/platformer/`.

Phases done: 1 (the core: tile physics, the jump, the camera, a test level, keyboard and touch), 2 (a living world: coins, bonus blocks and bricks, three enemies, spikes, lives, a checkpoint and the clock), 3 (power-ups, Bit on a skeleton with animations, the flag scored by height), 4 (three worlds of three levels, moving and falling platforms, three hidden big coins per level, the world map), 5 (a boss at the end of each world, saved progress that opens the levels in turn, the title, pause and end screens).

- `src/level.ts`: levels as text, one character per tile (`#` ground, `X` block, `B` brick, `?` bonus, `!` power block, `*` star block, `^` spikes) and things placed on it (`o` coin, `$` big coin, `g` walker, `k` shell, `v` flyer, `C` checkpoint, `S` start, `F` flag, `M` the boss, `G` an arena gate, `H` a gate that closes behind Bit), top row first; the grid has y going up and a wall left of column 0. Platforms: a run of `=` (with `-` beside it on its row it slides along that track, with `|` above or below it rides up and down; without a track it stays put) and a run of `~` (falls a moment after Bit lands on it). Each level has a theme (meadow, caves or clouds).
- `src/levels.ts`: the nine levels in three worlds (Prado Verde, Cavernas de Cristal, Fábrica nas Nuvens), each written as pieces of text side by side, bottom-aligned, ending with the same staircase to the flag; the third level of each world ends in the boss's arena instead. Caves have a low ceiling; the cloud factory is girders over the sky.
- `src/platforms.ts`: platforms Bit jumps up through and lands on: moving ones ease back and forth along their track (2.4 m/s at most) and carry Bit; falling ones shake for 0.45 s after Bit lands, then drop. The hero's step with the platforms (move, carry, step, land) is shared with the bot.
- `src/boss.ts`: the bosses, one per world, three stomps each, quicker after each one, flashing (no harm either way) for 1.3 s after a stomp: Rei Bolota (a crowned slime: walks at Bit, crouches, leaps at him), Geodo (a rock golem with crystals: walks, stops and fires shards, low ones to jump and high ones to stay under, more per volley as it weakens) and Turbina (a drone: flies out of reach dropping bolts, then swoops down and rests on the floor, where it can be stomped). The fight starts once Bit is in the arena (a gate closes behind him) and the gates open when the boss is beaten (1000 points a stomp, 5000 more for the last).
- `src/progress.ts`: what the player has done, kept in the browser (`localStorage`): each level finished, its big coins found and its best score; a level opens once the one before is finished.
- `src/themes.ts`: each world's colours (sky, ground, blocks, light, platforms) and scenery.
- `src/world.ts`: a level being played: coins (200 points; 100 give a life), bonus blocks (a coin, then used up), blocks bumped from below knock off the enemy on top, spikes, enemies that wake as the camera reaches them (Bolota walks and is squashed by a stomp; Casco hides in its shell when stomped and slides when kicked, knocking out other enemies; Zumbido hovers up and down), stomps in a row score 100, 200, 400, 800, then 1000, a bounce higher with the button held, three lives, a checkpoint to come back to, a 300 s clock, and the time left as points at the flag. Power-ups: a power block gives a battery (big Bit: 0.95 × 1.5 m, breaks bricks from below, a hit only costs the power and then 2 s without harm) or, for big Bit, a thruster (a fresh press of run shoots a spark that bounces along the ground and knocks out enemies, two at a time); a star block gives 8 s in which Bit knocks out whatever it touches; a new try starts small. The flag scores by the height Bit touches the pole (100 to 5000). Three big coins per level (2000 points each; found ones stay found on the next try). Events for sounds and effects.
- `src/physics.ts`: boxes moving through the grid one axis at a time at a fixed 1/120 s step: walls, landing, the tile bumped with the head, and slipping past a corner the head only clips (up to 0.22 m).
- `src/hero.ts`: the hero's moves: walk 6 m/s, run 9.5 m/s, quick turns, a jump from about 1.4 tiles (a tap) to 4.3 tiles (held), heavier gravity falling, coyote time (0.1 s) and a jump buffer (0.12 s).
- `src/bit.ts`: Bit's skeleton (a joint per entity: hips, body, antenna, arms, hands, legs, feet) and six clips sampled from poses and played by the engine's Animator: Idle, Run (its speed follows the hero's), Jump, Fall, Die and Victory.
- `src/camera.ts`: follows across with a dead zone, looks ahead the way the hero runs, never scrolls back (so the hero cannot go back either), except while the hero rides a platform sliding back, and only follows up and down where the hero lands, or far off; it climbs only when the hero stands well above the start, so the ground stays in view on low platforms. On narrow screens (a phone held upright) the hero always keeps at least 3.25 m of view ahead, and the camera climbs at once before the head goes under the HUD.
- `src/bot.ts`: a simple player that runs and jumps at walls, gaps, spikes and enemies, and, against a boss, plays each move (left, still or right, with no jump, a short or a full one) a second ahead and picks the one that stomps soonest without getting hurt; elsewhere it looks before leaping (it plays the jump with the same physics and the platforms moving first, trying a running, a walking and a short jump, and only jumps if it lands with room to stop, off spikes); it stops at an edge to wait, for a platform to come by; a test has it finish every level without dying, and `?auto` lets it play in the browser.
- `src/input.ts`: keyboard (arrows or A/D, space/W/↑ to jump, Shift or J to run) and touch (a pad whose halves are left and right, and A and B buttons that work as one area, so a thumb rolled from B onto A jumps without being lifted and keeps running) as one input state; a jump press is kept until a step uses it.
- `src/look.ts`: the tiles that never change (ground, blocks, spikes) merged into one mesh per kind per 16 columns, hidden off screen; canvas-drawn textures in the world's colours; the rails moving platforms run on; the sky and the world's scenery (hills; glowing crystals and stalactites; clouds, a sea of cloud below and factory towers), the light; the flag (it comes down with Bit); Bit's meshes hung on its skeleton, with a band that turns orange with the thruster and a shell that cycles colours with the star.
- `src/things.ts`: what changes, one entity each (the engine uploads a mesh once): bricks and bonus blocks with a hop when bumped, spinning coins and the one popping out of a block, the three enemies (squashed, flipped, hidden in the shell, wings beating), the checkpoint flags, the power-ups (a battery, a little rocket, a star), sparks and the bits of broken bricks, the big coins (a gold coin with a star) and the platforms (falling ones shake).
- `src/main.ts`: the title screen (the bot plays the first level behind it), the pause (❚❚, Esc or P, and when the page is hidden: continue, restart, map), the end screen after the last level (levels finished, big coins, total of the best scores), the world map (locked levels greyed out; a card per world, its levels down a path, with the big coins found and the best score; arrows and Enter, or a tap), a fresh scene for each level, the loop (fixed steps), the camera, the HUD (pause button, lives, coins, big coins, the boss's name and hits left, score, clock), the camera kept inside a boss's arena, dying (a hop and a fall, then the next try from the checkpoint), game over, Bit's clip, size (flickering as it grows or shrinks) and blinking while hurt, the flag (Bit slides down the pole with it, hops off and celebrates; then the score); `window.platformer` exposes hooks for tests.

Page options: `?level=N` starts level N (0 to 8) without the title or the map, `?unlock` opens every level, `?touch` shows the touch buttons on any screen, `?auto` lets the bot play, `?debug` shows the frame rate, `?quality=low|medium|high`.
